Code Gaon

Running

A free coding programme for government-school children in Kandrauli, Haryana, and the platform it runs on.

Role
Founder, lead instructor, developer
Where
Kandrauli, Haryana
Stack
  • Offline-first web app
  • WhatsApp

Problem

Children at the government school in my village had no way to learn coding. Power cuts are normal, and many students do not have smartphones.

What I built

I started an evening club, then a programme in a computer room the sarpanch gave us. Its platform works offline, tracks attendance and progress, teaches a Hindi curriculum, and sends assignments over WhatsApp.

Result

Classmates I recruited teach as volunteers. One of the first students kept it running during my board exams and now studies computer science.

Bloom Back

Closed testing on Google Play

An Android app that links temples' leftover flowers and spare clothes with people who can use them.

Role
Solo designer and developer
Stack
  • Flutter
  • Firebase
  • Cloudinary

Problem

Temples are left with used flowers, and homes and shops with clothes nobody wears. People who could use them have no simple way to find them.

What I built

An Android app where donors post what they have with a photo, and receivers browse a feed for each category. No money moves through the app.

Result

Built end to end, from data model to signed release, and published to Google Play's closed testing track.

Get it on Google Play

vaqeelsaab.com

Live

My father's law firm website, serving NRI clients in the US, Canada and UK.

Role
Technical lead
Stack
  • WordPress
  • phpMyAdmin
  • LiteSpeed

Problem

The site, which serves NRI clients in the US, Canada and the UK, had been hacked, and the original developer dismissed the warning signs.

What I built

Traced and removed injected spam links, rogue admin accounts and backdoor files hidden in the plugins folder, then audited the SEO and rebuilt the service pages.

Result

A clean site back under the firm's control, with a page for each NRI legal service.
