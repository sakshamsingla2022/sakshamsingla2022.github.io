About

A student from Haryana, India, who likes building things that people nearby actually use.

I've finished Class 12 (PCM, 92%) and want to study computer science. Most of what I build starts with a problem I can see close to home: a village school without coding classes, temple flowers going to waste, a family website that had been hacked.

Code Gaon is the free coding programme I founded for government-school children in my village, Kandrauli, Haryana. I built the offline-first platform it runs on, and the full story is on the Projects page.

Before that, I taught free computer science classes to about 200 children over two summers in Holi village, Ambala.

I also build apps. Bloom Back is an Android app that links temples' leftover flowers and spare clothes with people who can use them. ScoreUp is an AI SAT prep web app. For my father's law firm, I cleaned up a hacked WordPress site and rebuilt it for clients in the US, Canada and UK.

Away from the keyboard, I was my school's swimming captain, and I have competed since Class 4.
